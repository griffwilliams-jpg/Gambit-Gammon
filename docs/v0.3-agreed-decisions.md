# Gambit Gammon v0.3 — reconciled decisions

**Status:** Accepted provisional design baseline as of 22 October 2026. This document recovers decisions agreed in the design conversation but previously missing from the repository documents. These decisions are approved for specification and initial prototype work, **not** evidence of measured balance. Treat the original `reference/gambit_gammon_rules(1).json` as an unchanged v0.2 historical source; never silently substitute it for v0.3.

## Foundation

- Two-player traditional backgammon: 24 points, 15 checkers each, standard start, bar, legal moves, bearing off, and first to bear off all checkers wins. Normal move legality applies unless a power explicitly overrides it.
- Standard 52-card deck with no physical Jokers (the former Joker wild-card function moves to a collectible Jinn). Deal 5 cards to each player; maximum hand size 7. One **played poker-hand power** per turn; no reactive countering.
- Select the precise card subset to play: e.g. 2 matching cards can activate One Pair even if a third match is available. Played cards cease to be available immediately and enter discard at turn end.
- Baseline card economy: naturally rolled doubles draw 2 cards; a hit draws 1 card and forces opponent to discard 1 random card (if available); natural 6-and-1 offers optional discard 1 then draw 2. For a full hand, choose a discard before each draw; reshuffle discard pile when deck empties. Capture balance is a test hypothesis, not proven.
- Resolve a new card-triggered poker power before moving; cards gained through hits cannot be played that turn. A Royal Flush extra turn can be declared at turn end if no other poker power was played.
- Jinn effects are distinct from played poker powers and do not consume the one-poker-power budget unless specifically stated.

## Dice and lamp economy

- Maintain **qualifying random roll** separately from the **effective dice after abilities**. Effective dice that become doubles grant **four checker moves**. Only qualifying genuinely random doubles award lamp charge or consecutive-streak benefits. Dice values merely set by powers never qualify for lamps.
- Each player starts with **one free mystery Jinn lamp** to open before play.
- Each qualifying doubles result adds **one cumulative charge**. At **two charges**, award a lamp and reset the charge counter. A non-double breaks the *consecutive streak* but **does not erase cumulative charge**.
- Provisional consecutive bonuses: 2 consecutive qualifying doubles upgrade the lamp earned from that streak to **Enchanted**; 3 grant an additional lamp; 4+ is a jackpot concept **still to be designed**. Never require 5 or 6 consecutive doubles to access normal Jinn content.
- Treat opening roll, voluntary and compulsory rerolls, extra turns, and exactly when one random doubles event qualifies as **an outstanding engine specification issue**. Only one qualifying reward event per turn; a setting-power cannot farm repeated awards.
- A newly acquired Jinn takes effect after acquisition/at the transition to the opponent's turn, not retroactively on the triggering turn.

## Poker powers (provisional v0.3)

| Cards | Hand | Ability and interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 J/Q/K/A | High Card | Boost one die by J +1, Q +2, K +3, A +4, as one indivisible move with a legal destination; a normal die can only reach 10 using this power. Boosted results >6 cannot enter from bar or bear off. |
| 2 | One Pair | Reroll one die once before moving. |
| 4 | Two Pair | Reverse one checker movement using one die; usual destination blocking and hits apply. |
| 3 | Three of a Kind | Eligible checker movements may go forward or backward for the turn, with ordinary destination legality. |
| 5 | Straight | Reroll both dice and choose original or new pair, **or** impose reroll-both on opponent's next roll. Pending effect persists until resolved. |
| 5 | Flush | Shield one chosen checker against hits throughout opponent's next turn; ends after that opponent turn or sooner if self-stacked as in original. |
| 5 | Full House | **Caravan Exchange:** swap whole contents of two points within the same six-point board quadrant; one may be empty; both must be empty or solely occupied by your own checkers; at least one occupied; swap must have an actual effect; cannot activate with your checker(s) on bar; no hits, no bearing off, no dice spent. Preserve checker identities/enchantments. |
| 4 | Four of a Kind | Set one die to any value 1–6; manufactured doubles grant four moves but do not award lamps. |
| 5 | Straight Flush | Set both dice to any values 1–6, including manufactured doubles for four moves (no lamp credit from manufactured doubles). |
| 5 | Royal Flush | One additional full turn immediately after current turn; the bonus turn cannot chain another Royal Flush bonus turn. |

Standard bearing-off eligibility, exact-roll use, highest-die priority where applicable, and overshoot behaviour remain the rule. No backward bearing-off, no backward move off-board, no reverse entry from bar; re-enter any barred checker normally before using other checkers. The agreed provisional restriction is that backwards movement cannot take a checker out of the home board after bearing-off play has begun. Abilities cannot otherwise bypass blocked points or mandatory die-use rules.

## Collectible Jinn and lamps

- Each player can have **3 active Jinn**; their bonuses persist during the match while equipped. There is no separate unequipped inventory or account-level progression in v0.3.
- Rub/reveal lamps when awarded at the end of a turn; the starting lamp is opened before initial play. If slots are full, replace one equipped Jinn or release the revealed Jinn. A Jinn being replaced loses its attached enchantments unless a specific ability explicitly says otherwise.
- Exclude already-equipped duplicate Jinn if any other eligible choice exists. Single active copy per Jinn; do not reset once-per-match abilities by replacing then reacquiring.
- **Provisional standard lamp rarity:** 70% Common, 25% Uncommon, 5% Legendary. **Enchanted:** 40% Common, 45% Uncommon, 15% Legendary. Rarity probabilities need simulation, and an exhausted rarity tier needs deterministic fallback.
- Resolve simultaneous Jinn triggers in stable **left-to-right equipped-slot order**; prevent self-recursive/infinite triggers and disclose activations in game log. Slot order may become a strategic rearrangement feature later.
- Convert the original permanent doubles-streak abilities into collectible Jinn. Do **not** also award the old automatic permanent streak powers.

## Visual and engineering direction

- **Original** low-resolution/pixel-art aesthetic inspired by the playful feel of Balatro, with an Arabian Nights / Middle Eastern fantasy palette: indigo, burgundy, gold, turquoise, geometric ornament, magical lamps and distinctive animated Jinn. Do not reproduce Balatro's artwork, characters or proprietary assets.
- Keep checkers, dice, cards, Jinn slots and lamp progress legible; show legal actions, targeting, activation, triggers, expiry, effect text and event logs.
- Design the underlying engine headlessly and deterministically, independent of graphics, with explicit individual checker identity for targeted/enhanced pieces, reproducible random seeds, state snapshots, legal-action API and replay logs.
- Use automated seeded bots first, stronger strategic opponents next, and AI UI-testing agents after. Evaluate game length, win-rate asymmetry, deadlocks, move legality, power rates, rarity/lamps, reward farming and synergistic win rates. No balance simulations have been conducted yet.
- Exact renderer (Phaser/PixiJS/Three.js), UI framework, model roles and release format are **not** selected.

## Still genuinely open before implementation

1. Precisely which roll qualifies for card rewards and lamp/streak rewards after voluntary/forced rerolls and before/after first-player opening roll.
2. Clarify whether a rolled natural double modified into different values still grants roll-triggered cards/lamp charges (recommended yes based on initial qualifying roll), and confirm movement uses final effective dice.
3. Define all poker-hand evaluator details (Ace-low straights, five-card flush categories, overlaps/priorities, whether a player can spend otherwise-higher combinations on lower power).
4. Resolve technical timing for beginning-of-turn once-per-match Jinn use, shield activation without a reactive counter window, cooldown increments and Jinn retargeting when an enhanced checker bears off.
5. Define card drawing with an empty deck and empty discard, handling one-card draw shortfalls, how simultaneous card awards interact with forced discards, and precise ordered Jinn-trigger resolution.
6. Specify streak award order when cumulative charge and streak upgrades occur on the same qualifying doubles event, plus four-or-more-streak jackpot design.
7. Decide whether individually enchanted checkers with otherwise identical stack sizes count as a meaningful Caravan Exchange; protect all individual checker IDs.
8. Test rather than assume that the powers, capture-card penalty, 3 slots and rarity odds are balanced.

These are implementation-specification questions and balance hypotheses, not grounds to erase already accepted provisional decisions. Until settled, a coding agent must not invent new mechanics without recording a decision.
